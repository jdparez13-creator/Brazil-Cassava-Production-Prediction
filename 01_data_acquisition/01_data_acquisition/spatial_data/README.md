# Spatial Data

## Municipal Boundaries of Brazil

The municipal geographic boundaries used in this research correspond to the 2025 Brazilian Municipal Territorial Mesh provided by the Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics (IBGE).

The municipal boundaries were used to associate geographic information with the agroclimatic dataset and to produce spatial representations of the climate clusters and model evaluation results.

**Data source:** Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics (IBGE).

**Reference:** [IBGE – Geosciences](https://www.ibge.gov.br/geociencias/).

**Geographic asset used in Google Earth Engine:** `BR_Municipios_2025`.

**Note:** The 2025 municipal boundaries were used for the spatial analysis of the historical period covered by this study (2004–2025).
